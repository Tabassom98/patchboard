Import contract

Use the exact header in the sample CSV. Severity is 0–10; asset importance is 1–5; flags are yes or no. Dates must be ISO dates, never future dates. Vulnerability identifiers are scanner labels, not automatically verified CVEs.

Identity is asset + vulnerability. Resolved findings seen again reopen. Missing findings remain unchanged: absence from an incomplete scan is not proof of remediation. Older observations, duplicate pairs, and conflicting asset metadata are rejected. Asset names must stay consistent between scans.
